    The site doesn't open apps. It sends a photo.

    The site talks to Plumo through the channel it already uses: a photo of the transfer, the invoice or the receipt. The agent reads it, suggests the phase and only asks what it needs.

    Nobody fills in forms with mortar on their hands, and the expense goes in the same day, not at month end.

    In the morning, the decision is ready.

    You open Plumo in the car or at the office and see only what needs you, in deadline order. Decide with one tap and see right away what you kept from slipping. Everything is logged with who, when and why.

    From budget to the last payment, without letting go halfway.

    Money slips away between the budget and the last payment: an expense logged twice, a phase going over budget unnoticed, an invoice missing the site ID, paying ahead of the work done. Plumo follows every dollar from the budget to closeout, and doesn't let go halfway.

    Do I have to replace my spreadsheet or ERP?

    No. The idea is to start alongside what you already use and bring the data over gradually.

    Does the agent log or pay on its own?

    Never. It reads the receipt, proposes the phase or the schedule and waits for someone on your team to approve. Plumo doesn't buy or pay anything.

    Do I pay for the AI separately?

    Your choice: use your own Anthropic key or platform credits, with usage shown per site.

    We only use it to tell you about Plumo. We don't share it, and you can leave the list anytime.
